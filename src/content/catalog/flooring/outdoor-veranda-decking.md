# Teak Timber Decking Specification

Premium hardwood decking solution for outdoor verandas and patios.

## Material Properties
- **Natural Resistance**: Highly resistant to rot, decay, and insect damage
- **Weather Performance**: Excellent performance in sun and rain exposure
- **Aesthetic Appeal**: Rich golden-brown color that ages gracefully

## Installation Requirements
1. Substructure preparation with treated joists at 400mm centers
2. Proper ventilation gap between decking and substructure
3. Counter-sunk stainless steel screws for weather resistance
4. Initial oil treatment before exposure to elements

## Maintenance Schedule
- Annual cleaning and re-oiling recommended
- Inspect for loose fasteners annually
- Sand and refinish every 3-5 years based on wear
