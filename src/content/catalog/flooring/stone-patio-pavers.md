# Non-Slip Porcelain Pavers Specification

Modern, low-maintenance flooring solution for patios and outdoor spaces.

## Material Properties
- **Durability**: Extremely hard-wearing, resistant to cracking and chipping
- **Weather Performance**: Zero warp or distortion under sun and rain
- **Safety**: Textured surface provides excellent slip resistance
- **Maintenance**: Minimal upkeep required, no sealing needed for most porcelain

## Installation Requirements
1. Prepare stable, compacted substrate with proper drainage
2. Apply exterior-grade tile adhesive with proper notched trowel
3. Use tile leveling system for consistent surface alignment
4. Grout joints with weather-resistant grout and sealer

## Maintenance Schedule
- Regular sweeping to remove debris
- Occasional pressure washing for deep cleaning
- Inspect grout lines annually and reseal if needed
- No ongoing oiling or sealing required for paver surface
