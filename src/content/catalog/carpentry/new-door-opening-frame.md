# New Door Opening & Frame Installation

Professional structural modification for creating new door openings in existing walls.

## Technical Execution Steps
1. **Structural Assessment**: Verify load-bearing requirements and obtain necessary permits
2. **Layout Planning**: Mark new opening location and ensure proper clearance
3. **Header Installation**: Install structural header with proper load-bearing capacity
4. **King Stud Installation**: Add king studs on both sides of the opening for support
5. **Opening Creation**: Carefully cut the wall structure following safety protocols
6. **Frame Installation**: Install door frame with proper shimming and leveling
7. **Trim Work**: Apply casing and trim to match existing door frames

## Quality Control Points
- Verify header is properly supported and load-bearing
- Ensure king studs are plumb and securely fastened
- Check door frame is level, plumb, and square
- Confirm proper clearance for door swing
- Test door operation before final trim installation

## Safety Considerations
- Structural modifications may require professional engineering assessment
- Use proper support for existing loads during cutting
- Follow local building codes and permit requirements
- Control dust and debris during wall cutting
- Ensure electrical and plumbing lines are identified and protected
