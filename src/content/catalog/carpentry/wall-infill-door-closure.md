# Door Seal & Wall Infill Process

Professional door closure and wall infill for interior remodeling projects.

## Technical Execution Steps
1. **Frame Preparation**: Remove door hardware and trim completely
2. **Stud Installation**: Frame the existing jamb with pressure-treated timber studs at 400mm centers
3. **Insulation**: Fill interior void with sound-dampening insulation battens
4. **Drywall Installation**: Flush-mount plasterboard on both sides of the opening
5. **Finishing**: Apply mesh tape, joint compound, and sand to paint-ready finish
6. **Texture Matching**: Match existing wall texture and paint

## Quality Control Points
- Verify studs are plumb and properly anchored
- Ensure insulation fills entire cavity without gaps
- Check drywall seams are properly taped and mudded
- Confirm final texture matches surrounding walls
- Test for acoustic isolation if required

## Safety Considerations
- Check for electrical wiring in the wall before cutting
- Verify structural implications of removing door frame
- Use proper dust control during demolition
- Ensure adequate ventilation when applying compounds
